01 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

About me

Name Timothy Hong
Major AI & Machine Learning
Why I’m taking this course I decided to pursue AI & Machine Learning because I am genuinely interested in how humans interact with artificial intelligence and how large language models like ChatGPT and Claude function.
What I hope to get out of it By the end of this course, I hope to have learned how to communicate with AI more effectively, how to manipulate AI to accommodate the needs of myself and others, and how to build my own AI applications and systems.
A real moment when AI/ML showed up in my life As a competitive swimmer now in college, I have used ChatGPT to assist me in analyzing my goals regarding my sport, as well as create real schedules to support me in balancing my Drexel classes, swim practices, gym sessions, and swim meets. The Large Language Model of ChatGPT has helped me tremendously with organizing my future plans, and has given me professional guidance on how I should carry out my swim season, relating to when I should practice, how I should practice, what exercises to do in the gym, and so much more. Overall, I believe that when used correctly and purposefully, AI applications like ChatGPT have the potential to be one of the most useful tools that people in society employ in their daily lives.
One honest concern about AI A concern that I and many others may have about AI is how artificial intelligence video generation can affect society in a negative way. With the rapid progression since late 2023–2024, AI video generation has been used for many great causes such as marketing advertisements and social media entertainment. However, as video generation platforms grow increasingly popular and easily accessible, more and more people are utilizing AI for unethical purposes. Some examples of this are illegally using deepfakes for non-consensual content, falsely accusing an individual of a crime, or even hiding incriminating evidence. The uncontrolled use of such a powerful and easily accessible tool like AI video generation is an honest concern of mine, and moving forward, I am intrigued to see how artificial intelligence companies handle the boundaries on what society is able to create with their product.
Course objective I’m most curious about I am most curious about Outcome 3: “Describe how core AI techniques such as search and adversarial reasoning are used to model intelligent behavior.” Adversarial reasoning is when an AI faces off against an opponent desiring an opposing result. Outcome 3 matters personally to me because one of the reasons why I decided to pursue my major of AI & Machine Learning is to learn more about how humans interact with AI, which could fall into the category of adversarial reasoning.
A detail about me outside this class I fell in love with water and swimming at the age of 4 ever since stepping foot in the pool, and eventually at the age of 8, I joined a competitive swimming club team. To me, swimming is more than just a hobby… It has been my whole life. Currently, I am swimming for a club team in Philadelphia, PA.
